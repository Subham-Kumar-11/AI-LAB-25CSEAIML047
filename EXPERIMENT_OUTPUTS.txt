EXPERIMENT 01: WATER JUG PROBLEM USING BFS
Source File: Water_jug.py
====================================================================================================
Problem Statement:
Given a 4-liter jug and a 3-liter jug with neither having any measuring marks, find a sequence
of steps to achieve exactly 2 liters of water in the 3-liter jug (target state: (4, 2)).

Input Parameters:
- Capacity of Jug 1 : 4 Liters
- Capacity of Jug 2 : 3 Liters
- Initial State     : (0, 0)
- Goal State        : (4, 2)

Execution Output:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Steps to reach target state (4, 2):
(0, 0)
(0, 3)
(3, 0)
(3, 3)
(4, 2)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Analysis:
1. Start with both jugs empty: (0, 0)
2. Fill Jug 2 (3L) to capacity: (0, 3)
3. Transfer all 3L from Jug 2 into Jug 1: (3, 0)
4. Fill Jug 2 (3L) again: (3, 3)
5. Pour water from Jug 2 into Jug 1 until Jug 1 is full (4L). Jug 2 is left with exactly 2L: (4, 2)

EXPERIMENT 03: TRAVELLING SALESPERSON PROBLEM (TSP)
Source File: Travel_Space.py
====================================================================================================
Problem Statement:
Given a set of cities and pairwise travel costs between them, find the minimum cost Hamiltonian
cycle that visits every city exactly once and returns to the starting origin city.

Input Cost Matrix:
       City 0   City 1   City 2   City 3
City 0    0       10       15       20
City 1   10        0       35       25
City 2   15       35        0       30
City 3   20       25       30        0
Starting City: 0

Execution Output:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Enter the number of cities: 4
Enter the cost matrix: 
0 10 15 20
10 0 35 25
15 35 0 30
20 25 30 0
Enter the starting city (0 to 3): 0

Optimal path: 0->1->3->2->0
Optimal cost: 80
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Analysis:
Tour Cost = Cost(0,1) + Cost(1,3) + Cost(3,2) + Cost(2,0)
          = 10 + 25 + 30 + 15 = 80.

Problem Statement:

the final decision.

Input Leaf Values:
3 5 6 9 1 2 0 -1

Execution Output:
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Enter 8 leaf node values: 3 5 6 9 1 2 0 -1

Optimal value: 5
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Analysis:
During the search of the right subtree, beta evaluates to 2 while alpha from the left subtree is 5.
Since beta <= alpha (2 <= 5), the remaining branches [0, -1] are pruned, optimizing computation time.
